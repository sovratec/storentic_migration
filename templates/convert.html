How it works

  1. Enter the full server-side path to the folder.
  2. Click Scan to preview all Excel files found.
  3. Review the warning — Excel files are deleted after conversion.
  4. Click Convert & Delete All to proceed.
Tip: Multi-sheet workbooks produce one CSV per sheet — e.g. Report_January.xlsx with two sheets becomes Report_January_Summary.csv and Report_January_Detail.csv.

Safety notes

  • Only .xlsx and .xls files are affected.
  • CSV files already in the folder are not touched.
  • Conversion errors are shown per-file; other files still convert.
  • Backup your data folder before running on important files.
